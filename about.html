About Our Bike Rental Service

Welcome to MistyRide — your best way to explore the misty hills of Sri Lanka! We provide high-quality bicycles and e-bikes for all kinds of riders. Whether you’re here for adventure, relaxation, or sightseeing, we make your journey easy and eco-friendly.

Our bikes are regularly maintained and available at affordable rates. Located near Gregory Lake, we’re open daily from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Come ride with us and experience the beauty of Nuwara Eliya like never before!
